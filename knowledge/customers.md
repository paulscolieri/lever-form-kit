# Customers

Who matters and where they come from. Decides which fields the form asks for and who
gets alerted about what.

## Segments

| Segment | What they ask for | How they find us | Who follows up |
|---|---|---|---|
| e.g. Homeowners, weekly | mowing, cleanup | Google, referrals | office |
| e.g. Property managers | multi-site contracts | referrals | owner, same day |

## What a good lead looks like

- The two or three answers on the form that tell you this one is worth a fast call

## After the quote

- Follow-up timing if they don't respond: e.g. 2 days, then 7
- Channel: email, text, call
- What "won" looks like: deposit paid, scheduled, signed
